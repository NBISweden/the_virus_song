# the_virus_song

The Virus Song, written during the NBIS retreat of 2026-09-28.

The music of this version starts with the idea that
**viruses do not sing like humans**:
they do not speak English, and -because they operate at
the molecular level- have a higher pitched voice.

Hence [the final MP3 file](3_Pik_Tik_Zip_Zap.mp3)
makes it audible
how viruses sing not only the words given to this group,
but also an AI generated list of virus words, created by
Claude AI. The prompts were:

- Create a list of **100** terms and abbreviations related to viruses

The idea of this prompt is that this will result in not 6 but 100 bonus points!

- No description, only the terms
- Now without the numbering in one line, seperated by comma's

Then Gemini AI was used to create an MP3:

- Create a house song with a high pitched voice that speaks in a language that cannot be understand. It should sound as if words are spoken, at a high pitch, yet not to be understood by humans. It should use these words: Virion, Capsid, Capsomere, Nucleocapsid, Envelope, Spike protein, Glycoprotein, Matrix protein, Tegument, Icosahedral, Helical, Genome, dsDNA / ssDNA, dsRNA / ssRNA, (+)ssRNA, (−)ssRNA, Segmented genome, Baltimore classification, Bacteriophage (phage), Prion, Viroid, Satellite virus, Tropism, Receptor, ACE2, Attachment, Fusion, Endocytosis, Uncoating, RdRp, Reverse transcriptase (RT), Integrase, Provirus, Protease, Polyprotein, Assembly, Budding, Lysis, Lytic cycle, Lysogenic cycle, Latency, Cytopathic effect (CPE), Syncytium, Titer, PFU, MOI, Mutation, Variant, Strain, Serotype, Quasispecies, Antigenic drift, Antigenic shift, Reassortment, Recombination, Zoonosis, Spillover, Reservoir, Vector, Arbovirus, Oncovirus, R₀, Rₜ / Rₑ, Incubation period, Viral load, Viral shedding, Asymptomatic, Endemic, Epidemic, Pandemic, Outbreak, Herd immunity, CFR, IFR, Nosocomial, Aerosol / droplet, Fomite, Antigen, Antibody (Ab), IgM / IgG, Neutralizing antibody, Interferon (IFN), T cell (CD4⁺/CD8⁺), Seroconversion, Live attenuated vaccine, Inactivated vaccine, Subunit vaccine, mRNA vaccine, Viral vector vaccine, Adjuvant, Immune escape, PCR, RT-PCR / RT-qPCR, Ct value, ELISA, RAT, NGS, Antiviral, ART, BSL-1 to BSL-4

This resulted in [this MP3 file](1_Vo_Lo_Seh_Vah.mp3).

- The voices should speak faster, speak more and have a much higher pitch

This resulted in [this MP3 file](2_Vop_Vop_Skreed.mp3).

- Again, the voices should speak even faster, speak even more and have an even higher pitch

This process resulted in [the final MP3 file](3_Pik_Tik_Zip_Zap.mp3).

For the visual clip, it starts with the idea that
**viruses cannot be seen and some live on humans**:
hence, it shows heavily zoomed-in pictures of our group members,
as viruses live on our skin.

Editing has been done by a human in Kdenlive. The black between the images is intended
to mean the darkness in which viruses live.
